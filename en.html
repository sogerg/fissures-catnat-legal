Privacy policy

App “Fissures – Suivi & CatNat” (Android: com.sogerg.fissurescatnat; iOS: com.sogerg.fissurescatnat). Last updated: 11 October 2026.

In short

The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your cracks, readings, photos, notes, claim dates and insurer details stay on your phone. We (the publisher) receive none of this data.

Data stored on your phone

House address and municipality, year built, insurer and policy number (if you enter them), cracks, readings, photos, maintenance records, decrees you enter, claim tracking dates and settings. They are kept in the app's private storage and are erased if you uninstall the app. You can also delete each item from within the app.

These requests contain neither your name, nor your photos, nor your readings. Public services and app stores apply their own privacy policies.

PDF reports, the CSV export and the backup file are only sent if you share them, with the recipient you choose (town hall, insurer, expert…), through the phone's share sheet. The app sends nothing by itself.

Permissions

  • Camera: photograph a crack, its gauge or the reference card.
  • Photos: choose a photo you already took.
  • Notifications: local reminders (monthly reading, notification deadline, claim deadlines). They are scheduled on the phone; no server sends them.

The microphone and location are not used.

Disclaimer

The app offers indicative tracking only. Only an expert can assess a crack. It promises no compensation and replaces neither an expert nor legal advice.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect any data about them.
